Where the solar goes

Nothing is paid for surplus sent back to the grid, so a wasted kilowatt-hour is simply lost. Cutting panels, moving a pump or geyser into the daytime, or adding storage are the three ways to shrink that bar.

Is a small battery worth it?
Each row adds storage to the same solar array. Marginal payback is the extra cost divided by the extra saving it alone produces — judge it against a battery life of about ten years.

Estimate, not a quote. Savings are against BPC grid electricity only: no export credit, no net metering, no generator comparison, no financing costs. A fixed monthly service charge, if the household is postpaid, is not avoided by solar and is excluded from the saving.
